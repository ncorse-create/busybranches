01 The tree

Home is a tree, not a to-do list.

Seniors up top. Adults and children on swings. Pets on the grass. A badge on a face means something’s due. Tap anyone, and you’re in their care. Swipe up for the whole house.

  • A badge when someone’s due

    A count on the face for what’s overdue, and for what’s coming in the next month. Tap the face. Their care is the next thing you see.

  • Rings, hats, and a cake

    Spouses stay linked. Birthdays get a party hat, and a celebration once that day. Then the tree goes back to ordinary.

  • Full bloom when you’re clear

    If nothing is overdue or due soon, the canopy blossoms. The line under the title says so, in plain words.

02 Care

The schedule plants itself.

Add a child, a grandparent, or a dog, and Busy Branches seeds the care that’s usually due — from age, role, and species. Mark it done, and the next one rolls forward.

  • Well-child visits, dental, vision, vaccines, and flu shots
  • Vet exams, boosters, nail trims, and farrier visits for pets
  • Medications, with dose logging and a refill countdown
  • Optional school and camp forms, when you want them

Suggested timing is general information. Your doctor or vet has the final word. The app records doses you log — it doesn’t advise on them.

03 The house

One list for everybody.

Swipe up from the tree. The month’s care, across kids, adults, and pets, sorted by who needs you first. When the house is clear, you can see that too — and what’s next.

  • A single schedule, instead of five calendars in your head
  • Who did it, when a co-parent checks something off
  • Optional export to Calendar and Reminders
  • Reminders scheduled on the phone, with or without a signal

04 Family Board

A cork board on the lower bough.

Announcements, chores, and notes, one tag away from the tree. Chores never turn red, and they never say late.

  • Announcements Index cards on push pins, with a byline and a simple RSVP.
  • Chores A weekly rhythm, stars, and a small lead — not a lecture.
  • Notes Sticky notes in five colors, pinned or taped.

05 Everyday

The rest of the keeping.

The small records that usually live on a scrap of paper, still on this phone.

Medications
A supply that counts down, today’s doses, and a refill when it’s getting low.
Routines
Bath, teeth, potty, and the ones you add. Streaks for the long runs. Check them off from the Home Screen.
Sizes
Shoes, shirts, pants, height, and weight. Charts, and a card to share, are part of Pro once there’s a little history.
Document vault
Scan a card, search the words on the page, export a PDF. Reading the page happens on the device. A suggested title is a Pro extra.
Widgets
Coming up, Daily routines, and Current sizes. All three are free. Daily routines can be checked off without opening the app.
Grandparents
A read-only screen you compose: names, birthdays, sizes, and the announcements you choose. Never medications, appointments, routines, or documents.
The month
A recap of what got done, who grew, and what held. This month is free. The archive of past months is Pro.

06 Scenes

The tree dresses for the day.

Golden Hour is free. Pro adds six more palettes, and holiday scenes you can turn off whenever you like.

  • New Year’s Eve, Christmas, Halloween, the Fourth of July, Thanksgiving, and Easter
  • Evergreen, Sunset Orchard, Blossom, Twilight, Meadow, and Cocoa
  • Locked themes can be previewed for a few seconds
  • Scenes follow the calendar. The app never asks where you are

Privacy

Your family’s information never leaves your family.

There is no Busy Branches account, because there is no Busy Branches server. We don’t collect, receive, sell, or share your data.

  • On this phone

    Family members, medications, routines, history, and scans live in the app’s own storage.

  • Your iCloud, if you turn it on

    Backup and co-parent sync use Apple’s CloudKit, in your account. The developer can’t read it. Co-parent sync is free. Leave sync off, and nothing leaves the phone.

  • Grandparents see a smaller picture

    You choose the children, pets, and announcements. Medications, appointments, routines, and documents are not part of that view.

  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers

    On-device intelligence, in Pro, runs with Apple’s on-device models. Nothing you type or scan is sent to an AI service.

Questions

Before you download.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up, no password, and no email to hand over. Open the app and add someone.

Can my partner and I share one family?

Yes. Turn on iCloud backup and sync, then send a co-parent invite. You both see the same family, through your own iCloud accounts. Co-parent sync is free. The person accepting should start from a fresh install, before they’ve added a family of their own.

What does a grandparent see?

A simpler home: birthdays, current sizes, and the announcements you choose to share, with a way to RSVP. They don’t see medications, doses, appointments, routines, history, or documents.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is stored on the device, so the app works on a plane or with iCloud switched off. Sync waits until you’re back online.

Can I add pets?

Yes. Dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, reptiles, small pets, horses, or “not sure.” They get a branch, a vet card, and a vaccination record you can share — with a reminder to ask your vet for official documentation.

Does the app use my location?

No. Location is never requested and isn’t used. Holiday scenes follow the date, not where you are.

What happens if I stop paying for Pro?

Nothing is deleted. Pro features switch off, and every person, schedule, routine, and document stays. A theme you’d already chosen stays available.

Is this a medical app?

No. Busy Branches keeps the schedules and records you create. It doesn’t diagnose anything, and it isn’t a medical record. Confirm timing and doses with your own doctor, dentist, or vet.
